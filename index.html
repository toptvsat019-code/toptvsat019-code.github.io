👨‍🎓 Why Dental Educators Love JawAtlas

If you teach dentistry, or you’re a student trying to understand the jaw’s structure, you know that looking at a 2D X-ray is like reading a book in the dark. JawAtlas takes the same data and turns it into something you can explore from every side. Here’s what it does:

  • Tri-Planar MPR: See the front, side, and top views of your patient’s jaw simultaneously. It’s exactly what radiologists see, and now you can study it with your students.
  • Panoramic Reconstruction: That familiar curved view you get at the dentist – JawAtlas creates it instantly from a standard CT scan.
  • AR Slice-Through: (Augmented Reality) Hold your phone up to the patient (or a printed scan) and watch the internal layers appear right on your screen, overlaid on reality. You can virtually “slice” through the bone layer by layer. It’s like a magic window.
  • 100% Offline: No internet, no cloud, no waiting. Everything runs right on your device, so you can even do this in a cubital fossa room with no Wi-Fi.

⚙️ How It Works (In Plain English)

You don’t need to know this to use it, but if you’re curious: JawAtlas runs directly on your device’s processor using Apple’s Metal technology. That means it uses zero external libraries – all the heavy lifting is done internally, which is why it feels instantly responsive, even with large CT files. It reads standard DICOM files (the universal format for medical imaging).

💾 System Requirements (Quick Check)
